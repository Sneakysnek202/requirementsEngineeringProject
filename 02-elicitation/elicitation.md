## Stakeholders
1. Students
2. Lecturers
3. Equipment Technicians
4. Department Administrator
5. Equipment Manager

## Stakeholder Needs and Concerns
1. Students
   
   Needs: A simple easy to use way of booking equipment, to know what equipment is available.

   Concerns: Not being able to book or access equipment for when they need it
   
2. Lecturers
   
    Needs: A simple easy to use way of booking equipment, to know what equipment is available, access to equipment needed for teaching.

    Concerns: Not being able to book or access equipment for when they need it
   
3. Equipment Technicians
   
    Needs: To know what equipment is booked, and when its coming back.

    Concerns: Getting equipment back damaged, completely broken.
   
4. Department Administrator
   
    Needs: To ensure both staff and students in their department have what they're looking for and need.

    Concerns: not having enough equipment to meet the department demands.
   
5. Equipment Manager
    
     Needs: A simple easy to know where the equipment is, who has it and when its coming back.

    Concerns: Equipment being lost or returned damaged, or being taken without permission and auhtorisation.

## Unknowns
1. How much equipment there is, this could probably be answered by the Equipment manager.
2. Who is allowed to book what Equipment? is some specialist equipment staff only? This could be answered by the Department Administrator or the Equipment Manager.
3. What happens when equipment is returned late? this could be answered by the Equipment Technicians.
4. What procedures are in place to make sure the equipment remains undamaged whilst being borrowed out? this could be answered by the Equipment Manager or the Equipment TEchnicians.
