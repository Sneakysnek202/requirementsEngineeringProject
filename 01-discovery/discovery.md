# Week 1 - Initial Discovery
## 1. Facts
The college is considering a booking system for equipment.
Currently their solution is using spreadsheets, emails and informal meetings.
There has been multiple issues reported with this solution.
1. Equipment can be double booked
2. people aren't sure if equipment is available
3. its costing staff time having to check bookings manually
4. equipment isn't always returned when expected
5. students don't know who to contact for equipment.

## 4. Stakeholders
Students - availability, flexible booking
Staff - Equipment, priority booking
College - that the equipment is returned
IT Staff - that the equipment works, easy way to fix system if goes down
Suppliers - that the equipment is returned in good condition
security -  that the equipment is returned
Students might get upset with staff having priority with booking if they need a certain type of specialised equipment for a project and its never available because the staff have it.


## 5. Goals
The system should help the college show how much of one type of equipment is available e.g. laptops and help prevent double booking things.
the system should help the Students with knowing where to go for booking equipment.
The system should help the college with equipment not being returned on time.
